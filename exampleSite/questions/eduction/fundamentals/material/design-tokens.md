## Which of the following are categories of design tokens used across the Gurukulams Design System?

```markdown
Design tokens are the smallest reusable design decisions:

* **Color:** brand palette, semantic colors (success/danger).
* **Spacing:** consistent margin/padding scale.
* **Typography:** font family, size, and weight scale.

*Note: "Component nesting depth" is an implementation detail, not a design token.*
```
